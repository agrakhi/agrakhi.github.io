## Design Repositories

The designs from [Singh and Stufken (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1080/00401706.2022.2102080) are available [here](https://github.com/agrakhi/ParetoFronts_2levelSSDs). The top two designs from the Pareto Front are provided, and they have the number of runs, `n`, ranging from 5 to 25, whereas the number of factors, `m ', ranges from `n+1` to `2m`.
