## About

I am an assistant professor in the [Department of Mathematics](https://math.iitm.ac.in/) at the 
[Indian Institute of Technology Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/) in Chennai, India.  
We relocated to India in 2025 and are absolutely thrilled to embark on this new journey.

Prior to this, I was a tenure-track assistant professor at the 
[Department of Mathematics and Statistics](https://www.binghamton.edu/math/) at Binghamton University. I did postdocs at the [University of North Carolina at Greensboro](https://mathstats.uncg.edu/) 
with [Prof. John Stufken](https://sites.google.com/view/john-stufken), and at 
[TU Dortmund](https://statistik.tu-dortmund.de/) with 
[Prof. Dr. Joachim Kunert](https://statistik.tu-dortmund.de/en/department/professors-emeritus/prof-dr-joachim-kunert/).

I completed my Ph.D from IITB–Monash Research Academy (a joint program between 
[IIT Bombay](https://www.math.iitb.ac.in/) and 
[Monash University](https://www.monash.edu/science/schools/school-of-mathematics)) in November 2018. My Ph.D. advisors are [Prof. Ashish Das](https://www.math.iitb.ac.in/~ashish/) and 
[Dr. Daniel Horsley](https://users.monash.edu.au/~dhorsley/). In my Ph.D., I worked on finding optimal designs for several choice experiment setups.  
In addition, I worked in the areas of supersaturated designs, coverings, and pseudo-generalized Youden designs. My thesis is available [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bam-bEN9VrUKVEPGVzum1hRrAimAQJ_z/view).I was awarded the prestigious **Excellence in Ph.D. Research** award by IIT Bombay.

Prior to my Ph.D., I worked in the predictive analytics industry for about 4 years at 
[EXL Services](https://www.exlservice.com/) and 
[American Express](https://www.americanexpress.com/). While at AmEx, I earned the **Chairman's Excellence Award for Innovation** in 2013.

My research focuses on screening experiments, discrete choice experiments, and subdata selection for high-dimensional data.

I serve as an Associate Editor for 
[Journal of Agricultural, Biological and Environmental Statistics](https://link.springer.com/journal/13253) and 
[Journal of Statistical Theory and Practice](https://link.springer.com/journal/42519).
